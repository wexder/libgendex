libgendex runs one writer with persistent index/cache and library storage.
The first bootstrap runs in the background. Health probes do not wait for indexing to finish.

Watch progress:
  kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} deployment/{{ include "libgendex.fullname" . }} -f

Access the service locally:
  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} service/{{ include "libgendex.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  http://localhost:8080

Chart-created PVCs with persistence.*.retain=true survive helm uninstall.
To reuse them in a new release, configure persistence.*.existingClaim.
